# Reference Literature Collection

**Project:** On-Device Multilingual Zero-Shot Voice Cloning Using IndicF5 and Google LiteRT  
**Collection:** 21 main papers and 5 supplementary papers  
**Ordering:** Papers listed in priority order

This collection covers the foundations of IndicF5 and F5-TTS, multilingual voice cloning, efficient speech generation, model compression, and LiteRT/Android deployment. The first seven references are the most closely related to the project.

## IndicF5 model reference

The underlying IndicF5 model is provided by **AI4Bharat** as a software/model release, separate from the 26 papers: [official repository](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicF5) · [model card](https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/IndicF5).

**Note:** Conference papers and preprints are included for their direct technical relevance and are identified separately from peer-reviewed journal articles. The RIS beamforming paper uses LiteRT for Microcontrollers, while the Android papers use TensorFlow Lite on phones.
